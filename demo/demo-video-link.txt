# PRAANJEEV Demo

## 🎥 Demo Video

PRAANJEEV — AI Emergency Response & Hospital Coordination System demonstrates how AI can help identify the right hospital during a medical emergency based on patient condition, hospital capabilities, available resources, verification, distance and estimated travel time.

The demo also shows the emergency coordination workflow, smart routing and hospital pre-alert process.

## 🎬 Demo Requirements

The demo video:

- Shows the PRAANJEEV application running
- Demonstrates the emergency-response workflow
- Shows at least one key feature end-to-end
- Demonstrates AI-powered emergency assessment and hospital matching
- Shows hospital/resource coordination
- Demonstrates the emergency routing workflow
- Demonstrates the hospital pre-alert concept
- Includes audio narration to explain the workflow
